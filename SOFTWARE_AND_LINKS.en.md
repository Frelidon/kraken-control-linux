## Supported NZXT hardware

- NZXT Kraken RGB 360 (2023, Standard / Non-Elite)
  - liquidctl device name: `NZXT Kraken 2023`
  - USB ID: `1e71:300e`
  - official NZXT specifications and product information: https://support.nzxt.com/hc/en-us/articles/47207322896923-Kraken-2023-Specs
- NZXT 2023 RGB Controller
  - USB ID: `1e71:2012`
  - listed with the included RGB fans and controllers on the Kraken (2023) page: https://support.nzxt.com/hc/en-us/articles/47207322896923-Kraken-2023-Specs
